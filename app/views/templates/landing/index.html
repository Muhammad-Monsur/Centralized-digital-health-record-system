{# 7. About us (last section before the footer) #}

About us

We are Team CDHRS. We built this system around a simple idea: your health records belong to you.

Hospitals add to one shared history, doctors read it only with your permission, and every access leaves a trace you can check. No more carrying paper files between clinics or repeating the same tests because a report went missing.
